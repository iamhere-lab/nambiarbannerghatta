About Project

Villas beside a national park : Nambiar Ultra-Luxury Villas is a gated community of independent 4 BHK G+2 villas off Bannerghatta Road, South Bengaluru. Planned across 50.4 acres at Bilwaradahalli, it sits just 180 m from the protected greens of Bannerghatta National Park.

Every villa comes with a private lift, double-height living and dining, a multipurpose hall, a staff room and three terraces for celebrations, wellness and yoga. A 50,000 sq ft clubhouse with indoor and outdoor pools anchors the community.

About Nambiar Builders

Nambiar Builders is a Bengaluru developer founded in 2009, known for villa and township communities such as District 25 on Sarjapur Road and the Bellezea and Ellegenza villa projects.

On Bannerghatta Road, Nambiar brings that approach to a 50.4-acre villa community beside the national park.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nambiar Ultra-Luxury Villas and where is it located?
It is a gated community of 4 BHK G+2 villas by Nambiar Builders at Bilwaradahalli, off Bannerghatta Road in South Bengaluru, about 180 m from the greens of Bannerghatta National Park.
What villa sizes are available?
Villas of 3,732 to 4,638 sq ft on 35×55 (1,925 sq ft) and 40×63 (2,520 sq ft) plots, in east- and west-facing options.
What is the starting price?
Approximate all-inclusive prices start at ₹5.53 Cr* for the 35×55 west-facing villa and go up to ₹7.10 Cr* onwards for the 40×63 east-facing villa. Ask for the complete cost sheet for exact figures.
How big is the project and the clubhouse?
The master plan covers 50.4 acres, with Phase 1 on 31.45 acres. The clubhouse is 50,000 sq ft (70,000 sq ft with basement parking), with 25 indoor amenities and 18 outdoor amenities across the community.
What is the RERA number and possession date?
The project is open for expressions of interest (EOI). The RERA registration number and possession timeline will be shared by the sales team as soon as they are issued.
